Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 November 2026

The short version

The Wise Dragons is an offline-first app made by Volcanic 5D Design. There are no accounts. There is no tracking and no analytics. What you do in the app stays on the phone. It does not leave the device.

What stays on your device

The app can remember a few things so it can pick up where you left off. These stay on the phone only:

  • which dragon world was chosen
  • choices such as card answers and feelings
  • reward cards and your Weather Garden layout
  • display settings such as bigger text and calm motion

This information is not sent to Volcanic 5D Design or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

What the app does not do

  • No sign-up, login or account
  • No tracking or analytics
  • No ads
  • No sending of your choices, feelings or other app information off the phone

TikTok

The app includes a link to TikTok, @wisedragonspirit. TikTok is for ages 13 and over, so the link sits behind a grown-up maths check. A grown-up answers a maths question before the link can open. If it is opened, you leave the app and go to TikTok. TikTok is a different service, with its own rules.

Help

"Get help now" shows helpline numbers you can tap to call. The call is made by your phone. The Wise Dragons does not receive the call or see what is said.

This website

This page is the privacy policy for The Wise Dragons app. This website does not ask you to make an account, and it does not use tracking or analytics.
