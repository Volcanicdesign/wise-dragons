Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

The short version

The Wise Dragons does not collect, share or sell any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you do in the app stays on your own device.

What the app keeps on your device

To remember where you got to, the app saves a few things in private storage on your phone or tablet:

  • which dragon world (age group) was chosen
  • reward cards found in games and your Weather Garden layout
  • display settings such as bigger text and calm motion

This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

What the app does not do

  • No sign-up, login or account
  • No collection of names, email addresses, location, photos, contacts or device identifiers
  • No advertising, and no third-party advertising or analytics tools
  • No chat with other people and no live AI chat
  • No internet connection is needed to use the app

Children

The app is designed for families, including children aged 6 and over. Because we collect no personal information, we do not collect personal information from children. A grown-up check appears before any purchase or any link that leaves the app.

Help and outside links

"Get help now" lists helplines you can contact. If you choose to call or visit one, that service's own privacy policy applies.

This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics or trackers. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may keep basic technical logs (such as IP addresses) for security, as described in GitHub's own privacy statement.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
